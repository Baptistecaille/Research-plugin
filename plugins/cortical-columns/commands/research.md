# /research

Your research companion for thesis work on cortical columns.

## Available Workflows

### /find-papers — Discover New Papers
Search arXiv for papers relevant to cortical column research. Quick-scans results for relevance and pushes findings to your Obsidian vault.

**Use when:** "Find papers about [topic]", "Search for papers on [topic]", "Discover papers about [topic]"

### /analyze-paper — Deep Paper Analysis
Perform deep methodology analysis of a specific paper (arXiv link, PDF, or paper ID). Creates a detailed analysis note in your Obsidian vault with thesis connections.

**Use when:** "Analyze this paper: [link]", "Deep analysis of [paper]", "Review this paper's methodology"

### /new-papers — What's New Since Last Time
Scan for new papers published since your last check in relevant arXiv categories (cs.AI, cs.NE, q-bio.NC, cs.LG, cs.CL, stat.ML).

**Use when:** "What's new since last time?", "Scan for new papers", "Check for new publications"

### /map-literature — Gap Analysis
Analyze your existing Obsidian research vault, identify gaps in your literature review, and suggest papers to fill them.

**Use when:** "Map my literature", "What gaps do I have?", "Evaluate your paper collection"

## Quick Start

1. Start with `/find-papers` to discover foundational papers
2. Use `/analyze-paper` for deep dives on important papers
3. Run `/new-papers` periodically to stay current
4. Use `/map-literature` to identify gaps in your collection
